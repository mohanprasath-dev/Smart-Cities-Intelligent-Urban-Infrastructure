How UrbanPulse Works

From sensor deployment to actionable insight — our end-to-end platform connects physical infrastructure to intelligent decision-making.

📡
Step 01

Sense & Collect

Thousands of IoT sensors embedded in roads, buildings, utilities, and transit systems collect real-time data on everything from air quality to traffic flow.

🧠
Step 02

Analyze & Predict

Our AI engine processes millions of data points per second, identifying patterns, predicting failures, and optimizing resource allocation across the city.

⚡
Step 03

Act & Optimize

Automated responses adjust traffic signals, reroute energy, dispatch maintenance crews, and alert citizens — all in real-time for maximum efficiency.
